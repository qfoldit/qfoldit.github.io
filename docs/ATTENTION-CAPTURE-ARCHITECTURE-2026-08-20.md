# qFoldIT Attention Capture & Pop-Culture Sovereignty Architecture

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status:** Architecture / strategic doctrine  
**Scope:** qFoldIT scientific human-in-the-loop R&D platform

## 1. Architectural thesis

qFoldIT treats human attention as a computational resource.

The platform is not designed as a conventional scientific visualization layer alone. It combines scientific objectives, game mechanics, narrative context, spatial interfaces, UAG/MCP orchestration, and deterministic validators so that human spatial reasoning can be applied to difficult search and validation problems.

The architecture adds an **Attention Capture Layer** above the existing scientific execution stack. This layer is an engagement and distribution mechanism, not a substitute for scientific validation.

## 3. Attention Capture Layer

The Attention Capture Layer provides:

- narrative framing around an otherwise abstract scientific mission;
- recognizable interaction patterns such as spatial assembly, routing, folding, matching, optimization, and repair;
- persistent progression, missions, leaderboards, achievements, and creator content;
- runtime portability through UAG so that the same scientific task can be rendered in multiple environments;
- an explicit event boundary between player action and scientific scoring.

The layer is analogous to a user-experience and distribution system for human-in-the-loop computation.

## 4. Pop-culture sovereignty model

qFoldIT may use third-party entertainment themes **only where the relevant platform and IP permissions permit the use**. The architecture therefore distinguishes:

1. **Owned narrative assets** — qFoldIT-created characters, worlds, brands, and original scenarios.
2. **Licensed platform assets** — assets legitimately made available by a platform or rights holder under applicable terms.
3. **Reference-only cultural motifs** — concepts used for internal design inspiration without copying protected assets.

The system never assumes that the fact that an avatar or skin is visible in a user's Fortnite client grants qFoldIT a transferable, sublicenseable copyright or trademark license.

## 5. Scientific task examples

### 5.1 Semiconductor spatial validation

A narrative wrapper can frame a chip-design task as a repair or routing mission. The underlying UAG graph can represent a topology, constraint system, or defect map. Player actions become structured spatial events. A deterministic validator evaluates the actual task representation.

This architecture can support missions involving gate topology, routing, thermal constraints, defect localization, or other engineering problems, subject to the scientific data and validator actually available to the enterprise customer.

### 5.2 Protein / peptide / RNA structural exploration

A narrative wrapper can frame a molecular task as a spatial construction or repair mission. UAG represents the scientific task, the selected runtime provides interaction, and the CAMEO/OpenStructure layer performs the authoritative scoring where the relevant backend is available.

The player-facing narrative is therefore a **human-search interface**, not the scientific model itself.

## 6. Scientific integrity boundary

The following invariant is mandatory:

> **No engagement mechanic is allowed to manufacture, alter, or silently substitute a scientific result.**

Every scientific output should identify:

- the task/reference identifier;
- the runtime/session identifier;
- the action/event record used for scoring;
- the validator and version;
- the result hash or equivalent evidence identifier;
- the evidence level.

This preserves auditability even when the player-facing layer is highly gamified.

## 7. Retention hypothesis framework

The hypothesis is that higher-quality narrative, onboarding, spatial mechanics, and progression can improve engagement with human-computation scientific tasks.

Current evidence supports the **direction of the hypothesis**, but not the specific qFoldIT claims of +450%, +600%, or >40% multi-month retention. Foldit research has shown that interface design, level selection, prior expertise, and gaming experience can affect engagement; a separate Foldit experiment found that a three-star mechanic changed play behavior but did not show a retention improvement. These findings justify experimentation rather than capitalization of unverified retention percentages.

qFoldIT should therefore run controlled cohorts with D1/D7/D30/D90 retention, task completion rate, scientific-signal quality, and player-to-contribution conversion as the primary measures.

## 8. Enterprise economics boundary

A scientific mission may use a corporate reward pool, but the reward amount is an input to the customer contract, not evidence of present qFoldIT revenue.

The architecture should support:

No fixed $150k-$500k mission-price assumption should be capitalized until commercial contracts establish pricing power.

## 9. Legal boundary: no "zero-CAC IP" assumption

The platform can reduce content-acquisition friction where a game platform already provides licensed assets to its users. That is not equivalent to a universal Fair Use shield or to a sublicensable qFoldIT right to use every character, mesh, voice, likeness, trademark, or narrative element.

The safe architectural rule is:

> **Use platform-provided IP only within the permissions actually granted by the platform and applicable rights-holder terms. Use qFoldIT-owned characters and worlds for all proprietary demonstrations where licensing is uncertain.**

This makes the architecture commercially stronger because it removes dependence on a disputed legal theory.

## 10. Strategic moat

The strategic moat is not any individual fictional character. It is the combination of:

- scientific task representation;
- UAG portability;
- MCP/agent orchestration;
- multi-runtime execution;
- human spatial search;
- CAMEO deterministic validation;
- scientific evidence records;
- creator tooling and content supply;
- enterprise mission economics;
- experimental telemetry for optimizing human contribution quality.

This stack can retain its strategic value even when a specific third-party entertainment license is absent.

## 11. Valuation interpretation

The Attention Capture Layer should be valued as an **option and distribution capability**, not as proven recurring revenue.

The layer receives positive valuation weight when qFoldIT can demonstrate:

1. material improvement in D30/D90 contribution retention;
2. increased valid scientific contributions per active user;
3. lower cost per validated scientific event;
4. repeat enterprise mission demand;
5. creator-generated mission supply;
6. legally clean content provenance.

Until those measurements exist, the valuation credit remains a strategic-option premium rather than a revenue multiple.
