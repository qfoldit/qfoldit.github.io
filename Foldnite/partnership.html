FLAGSHIP WORLD
MAS «SNEZHINKA»
SCIENCE · TEAMWORK · DISCOVERY
THE SHARED OPPORTUNITY

A new category of educational game experience.

Foldnite connects the depth of scientific and engineering environments with the engagement, cooperation and expressive power of modern game worlds. MAS Snezhinka provides a tangible foundation for this experience.

THE PILOT WORLD

MAS Snezhinka — from station exploration to missions.

Participants become a research team. They explore the station's connected systems, take on roles and solve a challenge in which every action affects the shared situation.

Energy

Decisions on generation, storage and energy distribution.

Arctic logistics

Rovers, drones, routes and limited resources.

Scientific laboratories

Observations, experiments, biology and life-support systems.

LEARNING DESIGN

Nine principles become playable mechanics.

Foldnite scenarios can be designed around the World of Knowledge of Sirius principles and translated into observable player experiences.

01AccessibilityEntry experiences for different levels.
02First-cause thinkingExplore physical causes.
03Social learningDistributed roles and collaboration.
04Engagement through playGoals, progression and feedback.
05Belonging to something biggerConnection to meaningful scientific challenges.
06Responsibility through consequencesConsequences are visible to the whole team.
07Independent path choiceAlternative approaches to solving tasks.
08Living adaptive environmentFeedback and mentor support.
09Experience, not memorisationPractice, decisions and reflection.
PROPOSED PATH

Start with a focused co-design session.

The first step is to review the experience together, define the learning challenge and select a scenario that can be demonstrated and tested.

01 / DISCOVER

Walkthrough

Show the current world, laboratories and interactive mechanics.

02 / ALIGN

Shared challenge

Choose the audience, theme, learning objectives and participation format.

03 / DESIGN

Mission design

Define roles, scenario, tasks and debrief.

04 / PLAYTEST

Demo and playtest

Run a guided test and gather feedback.

05 / SCALE

Scale decision

Review outcomes and define the next stage.
