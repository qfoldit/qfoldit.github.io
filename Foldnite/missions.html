EXAMPLE: ARCTIC STATION

Mission: keep the station running through a storm.

Weather conditions are changing. Energy is limited. A remote laboratory needs power, and the research team must reach an observation site.

Energy EngineerBalances generation, storage and demand.
Research ScientistDefines research priorities and observation needs.
Rover OperatorSelects a vehicle and plans the route.
CommunicationsMaintains the link between station and field team.
Mission LeadCoordinates the team and compares options.

EXPERIENCE & REFLECTION

Meaning emerges when decisions are reviewed.

After the mission, the team revisits key actions: which information they used, which alternatives they considered, why they chose a path and how the system changed.
