ARCTIC · DIGITAL TWIN
MAS «SNEZHINKA»
SCIENCE IN THE POLAR URALS
ONE CONNECTED SYSTEM

The station is not a backdrop. It is a connected system.

Energy affects the laboratories. Weather changes logistics. Connectivity shapes remote operations. Life support depends on resources and crew decisions. Missions reveal these relationships through action.

SEVEN STATION ZONES

Explore the station from its energy core to crew habitats.

Every zone brings its own objects, processes and missions. Together they form one scientific and engineering world.

01 / ENERGY

Energy & hydrogen

Wind and solar generation, electrolysers, fuel cells, storage and backup power.

02 / ENVIRONMENT

Climate & environment

Weather stations, lidar, permafrost sensors, atmospheric measurements and observation routes.

03 / BIOLOGY

Biology & medicine

Bioreactors, microscopes, plants, incubators, telemedicine and life-support scenarios.

04 / ROBOTICS

Robotics & transport

Rovers, aerial and underwater drones, remote operations and route planning.

05 / ENGINEERING

Materials & engineering

Prototyping, climate chambers, test rigs and materials workflows.

06 / CONNECTIVITY

Connectivity & control

Satellite terminals, server infrastructure, central control and communications networks.

07 / LIFE SUPPORT

Living & life support

Crew habitats, shared spaces, water, air, food, health and crew resources.

ARCTIC FLEET

Explore the terrain and operate beyond the station.

Vehicles and robotic systems connect the station's laboratories with the surrounding environment—from route planning to remote inspection.

All-terrain vehicles

Transport researchers and supplies, choose routes and navigate challenging terrain.

Aerial drones

Inspect facilities, capture survey imagery, monitor conditions and find observation points.

Underwater vehicles

Under-ice exploration, sensor inspection and operations in the aquatic environment.

MISSION EXAMPLE

Deploy a weather station before the storm reaches the remote site.

The team selects a vehicle, checks its charge, plans a route, establishes communications and sends a drone to scout when needed. The challenge connects energy, logistics, climate and robotics.

DIGITAL TWIN STORY

From a VR model to a scientific game world.

In January 2023, the virtual station was presented to Yamal Governor Dmitry Artyukhov. In May 2023, he personally tested the VR digital twin. Subsequent stages expanded the virtual laboratories, collaborative scenarios and the AI assistant inside the virtual environment.
