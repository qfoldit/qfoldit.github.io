HUMAN × GENERATIVE AI

AI belongs in the scientific workflow, not in a separate chat window.

In May 2023, the MAS Snezhinka virtual laboratory included an in-world OpenAI assistant using Chat Completions (GPT-3.5/GPT-4). Users could receive contextual help inside the virtual environment. qFoldIT continues to develop a provider-flexible approach that connects AI models to scientific tools and missions.
