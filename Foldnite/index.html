HUMAN × AI × SCIENCE

The assistant lives inside the scientific world.

In May 2023, the MAS Snezhinka virtual laboratory already included an OpenAI generative assistant using Chat Completions (GPT-3.5/GPT-4). Users could interact with AI without leaving the virtual environment. This approach became part of the evolution of AI-assisted scientific experiences.
